Introduction

About Me

I hold a Master of Science in Information Systems from Indiana Institute of Technology, USA, and a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Engineering. My academic and professional experience has given me a foundation in software development, databases, data analytics and emerging AI technologies.

I am developing an interdisciplinary research direction at the intersection of healthcare, information systems and artificial intelligence. I am interested in how healthcare organisations can adopt and govern AI responsibly while considering patient privacy, fairness, transparency, accountability, safety and human oversight.

My interests include responsible use of generative AI and large language models in healthcare, organisational AI governance, healthcare data protection, human-centred implementation and AI policy. I hope to deepen this direction through graduate research, interdisciplinary collaboration and, over time, doctoral study and an academic career.
